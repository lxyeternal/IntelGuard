
Generative artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming industries, enabling businesses to harness the power of machine learning (ML) to reshape customer experiences and revolutionize software development.
At this year's All Day DevOps (ADDO) event, Pallavi Nargund, Principal Solutions Architect at Amazon Web Services (AWS), will present a keynote titled "Transform your enterprises with Generative AI," diving into the exciting future of generative AI and how it's reshaping the business landscape.
All Day DevOps: A global event for DevOps innovation
Now in its ninth year, ADDO is the world's largest DevOps event, offering 24 hours of non-stop sessions to over 180,000 attendees.
Covering a range of topics including software development, DevOps, cybersecurity, and emerging technologies, ADDO has become a crucial platform for knowledge sharing and innovation.
Nargund's keynote offers practical insights into the growing role of generative AI.

The rise of generative AI
Generative AI, a subset of ML, has been on the horizon for decades. But today, it's reaching new heights thanks to advances in scalable compute power, the explosion of data, and cutting-edge ML techniques.
In her keynote, Nargund will explore how businesses across industries are at a critical inflection point, where generative AI is moving from experimentation to full-scale adoption.
Nargund will highlight popular use cases where generative AI has already made a significant impact, from automated content creation to advanced customer service solutions.
New AWS tools for building generative AI solutions
AWS has been at the forefront of this transformation, providing the tools and infrastructure needed for businesses to develop and scale AI solutions.
In her keynote, Nargund will introduce attendees to the latest AWS technologies designed to accelerate generative AI adoption. Whether it's leveraging pre-built models or creating custom solutions tailored to specific business needs, AWS provides a flexible framework for enterprises looking to harness the power of AI.
Looking forward with generative AI
For those working in DevOps and DevSecOps, understanding generative AI is crucial for staying ahead in today's fast-paced technological environment. AI is increasingly being integrated into every aspect of the software development life cycle, from automating code generation to improving security testing.
Listeners will walk away with a clear understanding of:

The current landscape of generative AI and its potential for business transformation.
Popular use cases across industries that are already benefiting from generative AI.
The latest AWS tools and solutions for building and scaling generative AI applications.
Practical insights into how DevOps teams can leverage AI to enhance their development processes.
