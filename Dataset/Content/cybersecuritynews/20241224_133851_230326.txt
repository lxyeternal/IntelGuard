
Akash Mukherjee, a cybersecurity expert from Sunnyvale, CA, is finding new methods to secure AI applications on a massive scale. His experience in defending against nation-state attacks and Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) is a central reason he is regarded as a top expert in the Cybersecurity world.
A founding member and the head of engineering at Realm Labs Inc., Mukherjee is a specialist in AI security and is changing how companies safeguard the control of access and risk from insiders and supply chain security in an artificially driven environment.
Expertise and Career Development
Mukherjee’s knowledge of Cybersecurity, especially in AI and infrastructure safety, is the one quality that makes him stand out from his colleagues. He has been in charge of such projects at major companies like Apple and Google, where he was responsible for preventing insider and supply chain attacks on large enterprise platforms.
At Apple, Mukherjee spearheaded the AIML security team, integrating AI-driven security measures across services like Siri and Maps, which impacted billions of users worldwide.
Before his tenure at Apple, Mukherjee served as Tech Lead at Google, where he was instrumental in mitigating nation-state attacks targeting Chrome, a browser used by over 3 billion users globally.
His career milestones reflect a deep understanding of both offensive and defensive security strategies, from ethical hacking to developing frameworks that protect against advanced threats.
Mukherjee’s journey into security started early, stemming from his love of mathematics. Attending the Mathematics Olympiad, his interest in applied sciences was kindled, which prompted him to choose Cybersecurity as a career.
Major Contributions
Among Mukherjee’s list of successful accomplishments is one where he was charging the construction of the SLSA (Supply Chain Levels for Software Artifacts) framework while he was at Google.
This was a cutting-edge project aimed to deal with the supply chain attacks which have recently been a major issue after the SolarWinds attack. Chrome became Google’s first software to adopt SLSA, setting the standard for secure software development pipelines across major platforms, including Android and Google Cloud.
Mukherjee’s impact on the cybersecurity industry is profound. His leadership in creating scalable frameworks, like SLSA, has influenced the security practices of global corporations, including Intel, Citi, and Verizon. His contributions are not just technical; they shape industry standards. SLSA has become a part of OpenSSF, one of the world’s most significant open-source security initiatives, and is now natively supported across major cloud platforms.
At Apple, Mukherjee led the modernization of the authorization framework for an ultra-large machine learning platform, protecting sensitive user data and model development from sophisticated threats like data poisoning. His leadership and contributions in developing Private Cloud Compute at Apple further cemented his role as a visionary in Cybersecurity.
Now at Realm Labs, Mukherjee is focused on securing generative AI applications by developing the industry’s first universal authentication, authorization, and audit (AAA) platform for LLM-powered chatbots. This work is crucial as enterprises increasingly adopt AI-driven solutions, integrating their intellectual property with AI agents and chatbots.
Mukherjee’s approach to AI security is innovative, building novel paradigms that prevent security breaches in AI systems that operate on sensitive data.
Moreover, Mukherjee’s expertise is frequently sought in public forums. He has spoken at top cybersecurity programs and regularly engages with customers, investors, and other industry leaders to address emerging security challenges. His work is particularly relevant as organizations grapple with the security implications of integrating AI into critical systems.
“Akash’s contributions to AI and cybersecurity are setting the tone for the future,” says Jason D. Clinton, CISO at Anthropic and Mukherjee’s former manager at Google. “His work has a far-reaching impact, protecting billions of users and laying the groundwork for secure AI adoption worldwide.”
Leadership and Personal Philosophy
His ability to encourage creativity and work together defines Mukherjee’s leadership style. Whether he’s at Apple, Google, or Realm Labs, he has consistently shown that he can create and sustain highly capable teams that can deliver critical security solutions.
His colleagues regard him as a person who can think carefully and strategically and anticipate changes in the industry while adjusting security strategies accordingly.
Raised in a lower-middle-class family, Mukherjee attributes his hard upbringing to the reason he has developed a strong work ethic and a relentless will to succeed. Mukherjee went to IIT, one of India’s best universities, and then to USC for his Master’s degree.
Future Goals and Vision
Mukherjee’s current mission is to build inherent security in AI applications. At Realm Labs, his goal is to make AI safe for enterprise use by developing robust security frameworks that ensure AI agents and chatbots are trustworthy and secure. He thinks that AI is the most revolutionary technology since the Internet, and unlike the early Internet era, security must be an integral part of it from the start.
In his own words, Akash says, “We have made grave mistakes when building the internet, it was built to be inherently insecure. We can not make the same mistake with AI. My mission is to design the security block as we are developing this technology of the century.”
Mukherjee has big plans for the AI security sector; the ultimate aim of his enterprise is to be the most prominent security firm that primarily focuses on AI applications.
In 10 years, he aims to continue shaping the future of AI security, driving innovation that protects enterprises and individuals in an increasingly digital world.
