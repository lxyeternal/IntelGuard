What Is An IT Asset Management System?
A set of business practices called IT Asset Management (ITAM) tries to get the most out of an organization’s IT equipment by tracking its status throughout its lifecycle and combining financial, contractual, and inventory data.
To ensure that each asset is used, maintained, upgraded, and disposed of properly at the end of its lifecycle, IT asset management (ITAM) involves the end-to-end tracking and management of IT assets.
ITAM ensures that your business uses and tracks essential physical and digital assets. The main goal is to ensure that IT resources are utilized effectively and efficiently.
Reducing the total number of assets in use and extending the lives of those assets also aids in cost optimization by preventing expensive upgrades. Understanding the total cost of ownership and determining how to use the assets best are crucial components of ITAM.
