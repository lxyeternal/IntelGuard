
Using a virtual private network (VPN) can do wonders for protecting your privacy and security online, but it can also slow down your internet connection. It’s important to find a balance between staying safe and enjoying speedy browsing speeds.
Let’s explore how you can have both with a fast internet connection while using a reliable VPN service. But first, let’s understand why VPNs usually slow down your internet connection.
How VPN Affects Your Internet Connection
When using a VPN, your traffic is first routed through the VPN server instead of directly to the website you’re trying to access. This can cause latency issues, which means that it takes longer for data transfers between two points. As a result, you have slower browsing speeds. Additionally, if the VPN server is far from your physical location, it can result in further delays.
Even if you have the fastest internet plan from HughesNet or Verizon, network slowdown is common for people or households using VPNs whenever they are browsing the web.
Tips on Getting a Faster Internet Connection while Using a VPN
Now that you understand how VPN can slow down your internet connection, let’s go over some tips on how to overcome this issue.
Choose the Right Server
The server that you choose to connect to has an enormous impact on the speed of your connection. When it comes to VPN connections, connecting to locations that are close geographically is key. This ensures that the data will have fewer hops in order to reach its destination, which decreases latency, leading to faster downloads and uploads. Choosing a server close by also cuts down on packet loss due to distance from the original source, which is particularly helpful when streaming videos or playing online games.
Upgrade Your Plan
One of the quickest ways to get faster speeds with your VPN is to upgrade your plan if possible. Your internet provider usually offers tiered plans so you can scale up quickly as needed—with just a few clicks of a button. Before making any decisions about changing plans, however, it’s important to consider how much bandwidth you need in order for all of your devices and activities (streaming movies, gaming consoles, etc.) to run smoothly.
Use Multiple Connections
You may be able to increase speeds by using multiple connections at once—this can be done by connecting multiple devices or having multiple users connected at once through one single account. By doing this, you are essentially widening your connection “pipe” and allowing more data through at once, which leads to faster speeds overall for everyone connected. However, keep in mind that this does require more bandwidth overall so it might not be feasible if you already have limited access or only use slower connections such as satellite or Wi-Fi hotspots.
Consider Split Tunneling
Split tunneling is a feature that allows you to select which traffic should go through the VPN and which should bypass it. This can be helpful in increasing speeds since all traffic that isn’t going through the VPN will not experience any latency issues. However, keep in mind that this does come at the cost of sacrificing some privacy and security.
Switch VPN Providers
Sometimes,  the problem may have nothing to do with your internet service provider and simply be related to the VPN you are using—the features they offer, the server speeds they provide, or other factors. In these cases, it might make sense to explore alternatives that could potentially give you better speeds.
When choosing a VPN provider, you need to make sure that you’re connected to a reliable one. Otherwise, you will be at increased risk for cyberattacks. It would be best to check the most trusted VPN providers in the market and compare them before making the switch.
Final Thoughts
Getting a fast internet connection while using a VPN will depend largely on what type of service provider and plan you have, as well as where you are located geographically relative to the servers being used for the VPN connection. By choosing the right server location, upgrading your plan if necessary, and utilizing multiple connections simultaneously where possible, you should be able to get the best performance out of your VPN so that you can stay secure without sacrificing speed or convenience when browsing online!
