Systems management encompasses various components and subsets of IT systems management.
System administration oversees many IT requirements, such as,
Application Monitoring – A subset of system management that monitors and manages the performance of applications.
Discover complex systems and help to manage lifecycles and service levels.
Asset Inventory – Helps monitor and manage an organization’s software and hardware assets.
Maintain control over versioning, patching, and licensing.
Log Management and Performance Analysis – This subset helps analyze system logs.
It also helps collect, analyze, and correlate system data and infer system performance.
Capacity Planning – Helps companies ensure they have sufficient resources to meet their needs.
Security and Compliance – The role of this subset is to protect the system from malicious activity.
It also runs tools such as antivirus, malware, intrusion detection, and data loss and prevention systems to help with regulatory compliance.
Network Monitoring – This monitors the network devices such as routers, switches, wireless access points, and endpoints.
Network monitoring helps the Manager quickly identify failures and improve performance accordingly.
Systems Management Tools monitor and manage servers, storage, databases, virtualization, cloud, printers, PCs, and mobile devices.

Automation – This enables automated backup and restores automated workloads or desired configuration state.

Human Resources Processes – Monitoring and management of the company’s employees.
Planning, recruiting, training, onboarding, and hiring are all processes that are part of this subset.
