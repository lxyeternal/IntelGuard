The rapid pace of development within the Autonomous Vehicle space is incredible and as such is hugely reliant on Supply Chain; within an ever increasing software dominated world — this needs to be done right!
AV’s will have far more stringent testing requirements than passenger jets, and rightly so, with many more journeys predicted to occur.
For instance NVIDIA produce an AV framework called ‘NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion’ that car manufacturers can utilise by dropping into a vehicle chassis.

NVIDIA supply software updates and makes those updates available publicly.
Hackers have a long history of downloading updates from vendors, performing reverse engineering and formulating attack vectors.
The NVIDIA Supply Chain really does need locking down to prevent this.
