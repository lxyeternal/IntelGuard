The SolarWinds hack was a significant event in the world of cybersecurity, as it demonstrated the potential for supply chain attacks to be used to gain access to a wide range of organizations.
In a supply chain attack, hackers target a company or service that is used by many other organizations, in order to gain access to those organizations through the compromised company or service.
In the case of SolarWinds, the attackers were able to gain access to the networks of various organizations by compromising the software updates that were provided by the company.
