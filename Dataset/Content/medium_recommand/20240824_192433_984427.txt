Is everything as it appears?
Welcome to the first of a series of blog posts, where we will be delving into the dark art of deception on GitHub, a trusted platform that can, unfortunately, be manipulated for nefarious purposes.
As the world’s leading Version Control System, GitHub boasts a user base of approximately 85 million. It has evolved beyond its original purpose of simply storing and sharing code. Today, it’s akin to an identity card for developers, reflecting their skills, contributions, and collaborations.
The sequence begins by stepping into the shoes of an attacker who has just created a new GitHub profile. They attempt to gain your trust and credibility through a deceptively simple trick.
In the following video, we unpack the first method in which ill-intentioned actors manipulate their GitHub profiles to deceive unsuspecting victims, making the actors appear more credible.
Watch, learn, and stay vigilant. The world of cybersecurity is not always what it seems.
