How Big Data Helps Secure Cloud Servers
Massive amounts of data can now deliver insights to help protect servers against malicious attacks. This, of course, requires for this data to be properly collected, channeled, and analyzed. In this session, Igor Seletskiy will explore the real-world use cases on how big data is being applied to security to help protect against wide types of attacks. He will cover the importance of the machine learning-based applications, its security popularization in the cloud, and what hosting providers can do to tap into these insights to better secure their cloud servers.
