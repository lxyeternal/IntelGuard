With so many ecosystems woven with different devices, the need for strong cybersecurity measures has never been more critical. Understanding the IoT Landscape
The Internet of Things (IoT) landscape is diversified and large with a vast assortment of devices ranging in complexity. These devices are made to improve efficiency, convenience, and automation in our daily lives. However, the connectivity that defines the IoT also opens the door to potential security breaches. As more devices become interconnected, the attack surface for cyber threats grows, making it critical to address vulnerabilities promptly and effectively.
IoT Security Challenges
One of the primary security challenges for IoT devices is the sheer quantity and variety of devices within each IoT ecosystem. Conventional patching techniques are often unacceptable for IoT devices that require high availability because of the temporary disruption caused during a system reboot or update.
Additionally, traditional patching methods can necessitate on-site visits, which may be impractical for the IoT devices that are deployed in physically remote or difficult-to-access locations. This would entail maintenance costs and logistical challenges for manually patching each device.
Moreover, many IoT devices have limited computational resources, such as processing power, memory, and storage – especially those that are built for specific purposes or have hardware restrictions. Conventional patching methods that require a lot of resources may overload these devices and negatively affect their functionality.
IoT Security Solution: The Role of Live Patching
To counter these challenges, IoT live patching has emerged as a proactive approach to strengthen the security of connected devices. Live patching means applying updates or fixes to a system without having to reboot. In the context of IoT, live patching makes it possible to seamlessly apply security updates and patches to IoT devices without disrupting their functionality.
