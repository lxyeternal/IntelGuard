
The TuxCare team has improved the accessibility of our KernelCare Enterprise changelog. It is easier to navigate and has now been updated to provide a more clear view of the changes in a more agreeable format.

With some changes introduced in our TuxCare website a few months ago, the changelog functionality that you could use to track changes to KernelCare Enterprise was left in a somewhat difficult-to-parse format. You had to jump between multiple posts for each different version, and you lost the ability to have an at-a-glance overview of improvements made over the past few versions of the service all in one place.
In response to your feedback, and implementing some pending work on our build system, we are now happy to introduce an updated version of the changelog you can find here:
https://changelog.tuxcare.com

It is now clearer what version introduced which new features in a single, complete, list. And the information will always be up-to-date, as it is being received straight from the code build pipeline.
We value your feedback and try to accomodate any suggestions that make your work easier and better meet your specific needs. Feel free to reach out and let us know how we can further refine our offerings.
