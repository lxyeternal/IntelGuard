
Are you passionate about technology and eager to make a significant impact in the world of Linux security, cybersecurity, or open-source software? Look no further! TuxCare and its parent company, CloudLinux, are currently seeking exceptional individuals to join our talented global team.

Our team is responsible for creating and maintaining advanced and cutting-edge Linux security and support products that are based on widely used technologies. As a member of our team, you will have the chance to contribute to the development of these products, help our valued customers with their experiences using these products, expand the usage of our technologies, and shape the future of Linux security.
One of CloudLinux’s core products is Imunify, a comprehensive security solution designed specifically for web hosting servers. Imunify combines sophisticated intrusion detection and prevention, as well as robust malware scanning, to ensure the highest level of protection for your servers and websites. With Imunify, you can confidently defend against evolving threats and keep your systems secure.
TuxCare, our subsidiary centered on enterprise-grade Linux live patching, end-of-life security, and AlmaLinux support, focuses on delivering top-of-the-line kernel security through its flagship product, KernelCare Enterprise. KernelCare Enterprise provides automated kernel patching without the need for server reboots. This technology ensures that your servers remain secure and up to date with the latest kernel patches, reducing vulnerabilities and potential downtime.
