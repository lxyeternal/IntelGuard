Why migrate DevOps to the cloud?
When considering a move from on-premises to cloud deployments of SaaS solutions like JFrog, customers typically consider a number of key differences.
Scalability: Cloud deployments allow teams to scale their resources up or down on demand, providing the flexibility to accommodate fluctuating workloads and ensuring optimal resource utilization. On-prem infrastructure typically requires significant upfront investments to handle peak workloads, and resources can also be underutilized during periods of lower demand, leading to wasted capacity.
Total cost of ownership (TCO): The one-time migration cost can be worth it when compared to ongoing costs of managing and maintaining an on-prem solution. Maintaining and managing on-prem infrastructure involves substantial costs, including hardware, maintenance, power consumption, cooling, and physical space requirements.
Developer productivity: The cloud unlocks seamless collaboration and remote access to development and production environments. It allows teams to work together on shared resources, access code repos, and deploy applications from anywhere.
Security and compliance: By running JFrog on the cloud, organizations can leverage the security, encryption, and compliance expertise and infrastructure of cloud providers. Maintaining high security with on-prem deployments requires continuous hardware and software updates and patching, as well as a high degree of expertise.
There are many other reasons – such as high availability and disaster recovery, time-to-deploy, modern CI/CD – that JFrog customers consider when thinking about migration. Our goal is to make the experience as seamless as possible, using lessons learned from helping thousands of customers successfully migrate to the cloud.
