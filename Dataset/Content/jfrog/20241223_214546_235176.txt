The JFrog Codex – Making an Impact
We stand by our Codex and foster a healthy culture of innovation in a market that is defined by rapid transformation. While we are many, through our Codex – we can successfully act and win as one. Agility – Change is an opportunity
No doubt the pandemic underscored the need to maintain our Codex – and I’m proud to say that despite the countless challenges and distance between all of us around the world over the past 18 months – we all felt connected at heart. During that time we added 300+ new employees across all regions; showed continued revenue growth and customer wins; and were recognized by industry trade and business publications for the solutions we deliver and our drive for success, including TechStrong’s DevOps Dozen, Dun100, SD Times’ 100 Award, and as one of the Bay Area’s Best Places to Work by the San Francisco Business Times and Silicon Valley Journal.

It’s this culture of innovation – underpinned by compassion and commitment – that I believe makes JFrog not only successful, but a wonderful place to work. I’m honored to be part of this group of talented people – each with the heart of a frog and the spirit of a lion.
