Effective Disaster Recovery (DR)
By incorporating DR planning into the DevOps pipeline, you can ensure you manage it along with your application.  This essentially turns the recovery process into an application deployment process. And JFrog’s Access Federation gives you control over access to all, or any subset of your services from one location by synchronizing all security entities (users, groups, permissions and access tokens) between the federated services. Once Access Federation has been set up, you can manage all security entities in the federated services from one place which supports setting up the security entities you want to synchronize across multi-sites.
So when the time comes, your team can use their deployment knowledge and apply it to the recovery process — automation used for moving applications from dev/test into production and vice-versa can be used for failover and disaster recovery.
