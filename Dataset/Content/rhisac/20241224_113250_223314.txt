
The RH-ISAC team is working hard to strengthen support for our members to share openly and actively within our trusted community. We’ve teamed up with Hunton & Williams to gather insights for overcoming legal barriers to information sharing.
We understand that companies are challenged to provide better visibility, understanding, and gain support to engage in peer-to-peer information sharing among internal business units, general counsel, or other stakeholders. Last week, the RH-ISAC invited Lisa Sotto, partner at Hunton & Williams, to join a member-exclusive webinar and share her thoughts on how companies can approach and overcome legal barriers to information sharing.
