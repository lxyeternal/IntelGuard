
The annual RH-ISAC Cyber Intelligence Summit was held in Plano, Texas on September 20-21, 2022. Summit is the premier event for cybersecurity practitioners in the retail, hospitality, and travel industries. This year’s event had nearly 400 attendees for two days full of presentations and networking.
The post-conference report is now available to download. It includes details about the event attendee demographics, as well as highlights from the keynote presentations. Download the report here.
Additionally, RH-ISAC has released an event highlight video from the event.

The date and location for the 2023 Summit will be released once available. Registration for the 2023 event will open in the spring. The event is open to both RH-ISAC members and non-member companies that qualify for RH-ISAC membership.
