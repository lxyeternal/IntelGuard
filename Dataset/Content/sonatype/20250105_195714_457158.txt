
The Sonatype journey started more than a decade ago, just as the concept of "open source" and componentized software development was gaining steam. From our humble beginning as core contributors to Apache Maven, to supporting Maven Central and serving billions of components annually to developers, to building the world's deepest knowledge base of open source health and hygiene data, to helping thousands of enterprise engineering teams automatically find and fix open source vulnerabilities, we've pioneered the best practice of software supply chain management and reinvented how organizations use open source to build applications.
Today, we're excited to announce that Vista Equity Partners ("Vista") is acquiring a majority interest in Sonatype, which means that our journey will continue alongside a powerful new strategic partner.

Why Vista?
With more than 70,000 employees building and delivering software every single day across 60 portfolio companies, Vista is literally living in the shoes of software developers, application security folks, and ops managers. They know first-hand the intense pressure felt by engineering teams everywhere to accelerate the pace of software innovation. They also know that every organization building software today is consuming massive amounts of open source, and must contend with the challenge that not all of these third-party components are high quality or trustworthy, and all of them age like milk, not like wine.
At Sonatype, our vision, strategy, and execution have long been focused on helping software engineering teams scale DevOps by automatically harnessing all of the good that open source has to offer, while minimizing the inherent risks. Vista, perhaps more than anyone else in the world, understands our vision and appreciates the strategic importance of our mission. For this reason, they are the perfect organization for us to partner with as we continue to grow and scale.
Customers, partners, and the Sonatype community
Day-to-day we will continue to operate the same as we always have, except now we will have the backing of a very large and sophisticated partner.
With Vista at our side, our energy and resources will remain laser focused on delivering innovative products, empowering customers, supporting our partners, and fostering a vibrant Sonatype community. Whether you're using Sonatype Nexus Repository, Sonatype Lifecycle, Sonatype Repository Firewall, Sonatype Auditor or the full Sonatype Platform, you can be certain that we will continue to deliver the same great products and world class support that you’ve come to know and love.
Also, in regards to the community, we will continue to invest in and cultivate our popular and long standing projects like Central Repository and Sonatype Nexus Repository OSS.

Our engineers, our product owners, our data scientists, our technical support team, our customer success team, our account managers, we're all driven to help you succeed and make building software easier and more secure. This commitment is stronger than ever during this exciting time.
