
Sonatype Learn — your trusted DevOps and Sonatype product training resource — is all new. We've launched an industry-leading Learning Management System (LMS) with updated courses, fresh videos, and a whole new learning vibe.
Dive into Sonatype Learn for a smoother, intuitive user experience with clever ways to explore and handle your education needs.

Key benefits
Sonatype Learn puts education at your fingertips, and provides access to the following resources:

Nine comprehensive courses to dive deeper into the subjects that matter to you
Bite-sized demo videos to walk you through specific tasks
Best Practices lessons for success
Refreshed and updated content
Plus, check in often for new Learn packages with fresh resources all tied together by common themes.
What's next?
What you see is just the start. The new Sonatype Learn has tons of features that we can't wait to start rolling out.
Here's a bit of what we have planned:

Product onboarding journeys: If you're new to Sonatype, we have your back. We're creating journeys for all of our products that help you get up and running quickly.
More and more microlearning: Everyone's busy — we get it. We're focusing on short lessons and videos that get you what you need.
User group management: Assign courses, get enrollment and completion data, and more.
Whether you're new to the DevOps universe or looking to level up with Sonatype products, Sonatype Learn is perfect for you. Best of all, it's free. Simply sign in or sign up with your My Sonatype account to get started.
Please note that while all courses have been updated, some did not make the journey to our new site. Completion and enrollment data were successfully migrated, but in-progress data could not be transferred.
