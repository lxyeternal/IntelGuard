On June 6 - 7, we will be hosting the Nexus User Conference online.
The conference is free because we want your entire team to attend -- no matter where you live. Every session will be live, but everyone who registers will have access to the recordings. The conference will also be very interactive with Q&A hosted on Slack.
We have an awesome agenda lining up for you too. Our CTO and co-founder, Brian Fox, is on the agenda. John Willis, co-author of the DevOps Handbook, will be joining us. You'll hear from all of our product owners, and you will also hear from our customers like Aetna, Fannie Mae, National Institute of Health, and Tomitribe.

We'll be discussing DevSecOps and cultural transformations. We shed new light on how to automate open source governance policies. Speakers from our customers will show you how to deploy Nexus Repository on AWS or how to use it as a private Docker registry. You'll also hear what's coming next on Sonatype's roadmap for 2018.
To join us, register yourself or your entire team at:
https://www.sonatype.com/nexus-user-conference
Have an experience that you want to share?  Our CFP is open.  We would love to hear from you.
