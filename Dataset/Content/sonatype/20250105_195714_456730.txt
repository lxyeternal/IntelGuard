
We are pleased to announce that Sonatype is an honoree in the Cybersecurity Impact Awards.
The awards are dedicated to shining a spotlight on Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia companies and individuals for their leadership and innovation within the cybersecurity industry. The independent panel of judges evaluated companies across categories. Sonatype placed in the "Large Company" category.
"This year's nominees included a broad spectrum of cybersecurity companies, including a firm that provides advanced red team automation solutions, quantum security, encryption and compliance support. These are the firms that should be on the short list of any enterprise seeking to economically reduce risk in the modern age. I have had the privilege of being a judge the past two years and appreciate the opportunity to review the submissions from so many amazing cybersecurity companies in this region. Once again the quality of submissions proves that the level of innovation and talent in this region is second to none," said Bob Gourley, CTO, OODA LLC.
