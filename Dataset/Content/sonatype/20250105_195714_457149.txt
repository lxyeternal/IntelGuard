
Sonatype is working on improving the overall experience when dealing with microservices, containers, and serverless development such as AWS Lambdas or Azure Functions. We would love to understand your needs. If you are excited about microservices, containers, or serverless development, please take a moment to fill out this survey. The survey should take 3-5 minutes to complete.
We want to learn more about the problems this type of software development creates and the reasonings behind the solutions you are using to address them.
We value your survey feedback, and we would also like the chance to talk to you. If you are interested in improving the Nexus IQ Server for microservices, containers and serverless development, we would also like to invite you to join a 30-minute call to learn more about your needs. At the end of the survey, you will have the opportunity to sign up to join a user feedback call with our Nexus IQ Server product manager to talk more about your interest.
