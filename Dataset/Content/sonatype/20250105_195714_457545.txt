
The CFP for the Nexus User Conference is now open.
Last year, we hosted the first annual Nexus User Conference. Over 1,000 DevSecOps practitioners from around the world tuned in for the free, live, and online event.
Sessions throughout the day allowed speakers a stage to share their own DevSecOps journeys. In turn, attendees learned new skills and approaches - like how to deploy Nexus Repository on AWS or improve the security pipeline - and then were able to ask the speakers anything in live Q&As on Slack. Our product and leadership teams also shared some perspective and a roadmap for the Nexus platform, including a reveal of new updates.

Whether you spoke or just tuned in, we hope the journeys you learned about inspired you to push the envelope when it comes to Nexus and DevSecOps. Now is your chance to do the same for over 2,000 other people around the world on June 12.
If you have a great Nexus story to tell, we invite you to submit a talk to speak here.
If you don’t know exactly what you want to talk about, or just want to relive last year’s conference, do not fear - we have you covered. Leading up to June 12, we’ll be sharing recaps of last year’s sessions every week. If you’re looking for some inspiration, be sure to check in or subscribe to the blog to stay updated.
