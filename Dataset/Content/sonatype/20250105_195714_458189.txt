
The welcome page in Sonatype Nexus Repository is a hosted service that gives access to timely updates and resources to help with your work. It's integrated with Sonatype Nexus Repository out of the box and is the first thing you see after logging into the UI.
image: D:/Desktop/malware/IntelliGraph\Dataset\Image\sonatype\20250105_195714_458189\20170802NexusInterface011.jpg

Periodically, the welcome page checks for an update to its content and pulls it down if available. This allows us to provide you with the latest information, even if you're not on the latest version of Sonatype Nexus Repository.
You may have noticed that we've made frequent changes to this content over the last six months. Historically, the welcome page has been a rather overlooked part of the Sonatype Nexus Repository experience. Going forward, we plan to change this, with updated content delivered on a weekly basis in order to provide you with the most useful and relevant information. If you have any feedback for us on how the welcome page could be improved, please fill out this survey.

Our revised version of the welcome page is shown below. In the first week, we saw a 10% bump in engagement.
image: D:/Desktop/malware/IntelliGraph\Dataset\Image\sonatype\20250105_195714_458189\20170802NexusInterface061.jpg

We had two hypotheses for this increase in engagement.

The 3.4.0 announcement
The simplified layout with fewer links
Two weeks after releasing this simplified layout, engagement is back to pre-experiment levels, making us believe that hypothesis #1 was responsible. The initial excitement around 3.4.0 has leveled off, resulting in a drop from those initial numbers.
It's still useful to note that engagement is exactly the same as before, even with half as many links on the page. This gives us confidence that those other links, while not preventing people from engaging with the page, weren't helping drive engagement either.

Going forward, we plan to augment the qualitative metrics shown above with a survey and user interviews led by Shade Solon, our Senior UX Researcher. We've put together a proposal for what the welcome page could become, and would love to get your feedback on it.
If you'd like to chat with us about this and any ideas you have for improving the welcome page, please fill out this survey and we'll be in touch.
We expect to continue shipping updates to the welcome page on a weekly basis. Expect to see more here soon, but feel free to reach out at any time with your thoughts and ideas. Thanks!
