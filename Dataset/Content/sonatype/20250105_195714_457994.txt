About Dr. Bill Curtis
Dr. Bill Curtis (1948) is an American software and organizational scientist. He is SrVP and Chief Scientist at CAST where he heads CAST Research Labs.  He is also the Executive Director of the Consortium for IT Software Quality, where he leads development of standards for automating software size and structural quality from source code.
Dr. Curtis is best known for leading the development of the Capability Maturity Model [1] (CMM for Software) and the People CMM [2] in the Software Engineering Institute at Carnegie Mellon University. He co-founded TeraQuest, a provider of CMM-based services, which was sold to Borland Software Corporation in 2005. He has published 5 books, over 150 articles, and in 2007 was elected a Fellow of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers for his career contributions to software process improvement and measurement.
